Questions

Clear answers before you begin.

Does QUANTUM AI guarantee profits?

No. Trading involves risk, and no signal, strategy or software can guarantee profits or prevent losses.

Which markets are supported?

The platform currently provides Forex and crypto market information and analysis. Broker execution features are limited to the integrations and account permissions available inside the app.

Can I use the platform on my phone?

Yes. The live platform is mobile-friendly and can be opened in your phone browser. A separately signed Android package is being prepared.

Does it place trades automatically?

Automation availability depends on the supported broker connection, account mode, provider approval and permissions. Do not enable live trading until you understand the risks and have completed demo testing.
